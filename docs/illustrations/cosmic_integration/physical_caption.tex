\paragraph{Proposed caption.}
Cosmic integration of a fixed COMPAS population.
(a) Metallicity-specific star formation per unit natural-log metallicity,
$\psi_u$ from Eq.~\eqref{eq:ci_illustration_formation}, where $u=\ln Z$.
Dashed horizontal lines mark the metallicity range sampled by COMPAS;
the formation distribution is shown over its full numerical normalization grid.
(b) The empirical compact-binary merger yield $Y(u,t_{\rm d})$ per unit formed
stellar mass, per $\mathrm{d}u$ and per Gyr of delay
[Eq.~\eqref{eq:ci_illustration_yield}]. The yield is corrected
for the population's uniform-$\ln Z$ sampling density. Grey cells contain no
selected binaries; positive values use the colour scale, without smoothing.
(c) Metallicity-weighted formation contributions as a function of formation
time and delay. Each diagonal is a fixed merger time in Gyr; integrating
$C(t_{\rm m}-t_{\rm d},t_{\rm d})$ over delay gives the source-frame merger-rate
density at that epoch [Eqs.~\eqref{eq:ci_illustration_contribution}
and \eqref{eq:ci_illustration_merger}]. The solid diagonal marks the present age in WMAP9;
points above it would merge after the present epoch. Formation is restricted
to $z_{\rm f}\leq10$, with earliest time $t_{\min}=t(z=10)$.
(d) Predicted observer-frame rates in redshift bins of width 0.1,
summed over binary masses. Both curves include the same comoving volume and
time dilation; they differ only in detection probability
[Eq.~\eqref{eq:ci_illustration_detection}]. The vertical axis
is logarithmic above $1\,\mathrm{yr}^{-1}$ and linear below it, retaining
zeros and negligible positive rates. Here $\vartheta=(\mathcal M_c,q)$;
the selection integral retains the dependence on binary properties before
marginalizing them. Colour densities in (a) and (b) are per $\mathrm{d}\ln Z$,
not per $\mathrm{d}Z$, despite displaying metallicity on a logarithmic axis.
Colour scales are independent between panels; lower extensions indicate
positive values below the displayed range.
The illustration uses 512 million initial systems, yielding 1,326,573 selected
double compact objects, with the existing Hubble-time, pessimistic-common-envelope
and no-immediate-RLOF selection. Cosmic-history parameters are
$(\alpha,\sigma,a_{\rm SFR},d_{\rm SFR})=(-0.3,0.35,0.01,4.7)$,
with $a_{\rm SFR}$ in $M_\odot\,\mathrm{yr}^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-3}$;
detection uses the fixed O3 approximation and SNR threshold 8.
Panels (b) and (c) are display histograms of the finite population.
Rates in (d) use the existing integrator's formation-redshift discretization
and unbinned binary properties, not quadrature of the plotted histograms.

\paragraph{Supporting appendix text.}
Write the cosmic-history model per unit natural-log metallicity,
\begin{equation}
\psi_u(t_{\rm f},u\mid\lambda)
=\dot\rho_\star(t_{\rm f}\mid a,d)\,p_u(u\mid t_{\rm f},\alpha,\sigma),
\qquad u=\ln Z.
\label{eq:ci_illustration_formation}
\end{equation}
Thus $\psi_u=Z\psi$ when $\psi$ is expressed per unit $Z$ in the main text.

If $M_{\rm sim}$ is the effective formed stellar mass represented by the
archive and $p_{\rm draw}(u)$ its metallicity sampling density, the empirical
yield, marginalized over binary masses, is
\begin{equation}
Y(u,t_{\rm d}) = \frac{1}{M_{\rm sim}}\sum_i
\frac{\delta(u-u_i)\,\delta(t_{\rm d}-t_{{\rm d},i})}
{p_{\rm draw}(u_i)}.
\label{eq:ci_illustration_yield}
\end{equation}
Here $M_{\rm sim}=N_{\rm initial}\langle M_{\rm formed}\rangle$, using the
existing study's normalization of $79.29365659505004\,M_\odot$ per initial
system. The continuum equations describe the integration structure; the
numerical panels preserve the implementation's metallicity digitization and,
for the rate calculation, formation-redshift rounding convention.
$\mathcal R_{\rm src}(t)$ denotes the rate density per source-frame year and
comoving volume, marginalized over $\vartheta$;
$\mathcal R_{\rm src}(\vartheta,z)$ denotes its differential counterpart.
No additional time-dilation or selection factor is applied after the detected
rates have been computed.

The metallicity integral weights this yield by the formation history,
\begin{equation}
C(t_{\rm f},t_{\rm d})=
\int\psi_u(t_{\rm f},u)Y(u,t_{\rm d})\,\mathrm{d}u.
\label{eq:ci_illustration_contribution}
\end{equation}
The source-frame merger-rate density follows along a constant-merger-time
line, with $t_{\min}=t(z=10)$:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal R_{\rm src}(t_{\rm m})=
\int_0^{t_{\rm m}-t_{\min}}
C(t_{\rm m}-t_{\rm d},t_{\rm d})\,\mathrm{d}t_{\rm d}.
\label{eq:ci_illustration_merger}
\end{equation}
Retaining the binary properties $\vartheta=(\mathcal M_c,q)$ until selection
has been applied gives the observer-frame detected rate,
\begin{equation}
\frac{\mathrm{d}R_{\rm det}}{\mathrm{d}z_{\rm m}}=
\frac{\mathrm{d}V_c}{\mathrm{d}z_{\rm m}}\frac{1}{1+z_{\rm m}}
\int p_{\rm detect}(\vartheta,z_{\rm m})
\mathcal R_{\rm src}(\vartheta,z_{\rm m})\,\mathrm{d}\vartheta.
\label{eq:ci_illustration_detection}
\end{equation}
Setting $p_{\rm detect}=1$ yields the complete-selection curve; it is still
an observer-frame rate integrated over cosmological volume, rather than an
intrinsic rate density. The illustration uses the full population to reduce
finite-sample structure in the histograms. It does not establish population
convergence or change the population used for inference.
